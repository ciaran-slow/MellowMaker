### Named Android scenario for #16 (verify finding, PR #49)

`FlatList` defaults `removeClippedSubviews` to `true` on Android
(`node_modules/react-native/Libraries/Lists/FlatList.js`). The
`ListHeaderComponent` cell is a direct child of the scroll content, and after
#43 that cell contains the `react-native-webview` instance, so once the video
card scrolls fully off-screen Android may detach its native subtree.

**Run this on Android during the #16 device pass:** open a guide with six or
more steps, wait for the player to reach `ready`, scroll to the last step so the
video card leaves the viewport entirely, then scroll back to the top.
**Expect:** the embed is still there and still playable — not blanked, not
reloaded from the start. If it blanks or reloads, the fix direction is
`removeClippedSubviews={false}` on the guide list (measure the scroll cost on a
long guide before adopting it), and it needs its own issue.

Nobody has seen this fail: it was derived from the React Native default, not
observed, so it is recorded as a scenario to run rather than a prop change to
make. iOS is unaffected (the default is `false` there).
