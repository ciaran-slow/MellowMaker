## Still on-device-unverified

### 1. The Android half of AC1 — UNMET, not provisional

> Owner device check: text and placeholder centred in "Name your pattern",
> "Search stitches", "YouTube link", and the counter rename field — **on
> Android**.

No Android SDK and no `adb` on this machine ([`smoke-verification.md`
§1](../smoke-verification.md)), so this has never run. The source-level
expectation is that Android was never the platform with the problem:
`ReactEditText.kt` attaches a `CustomLineHeightSpan`, which splits the leading
evenly (`fm.ascent -= ceil(leading / 2)`, `fm.descent += floor(leading / 2)`),
so the line was already centred there. Removing the `lineHeight` removes the
span; `textAlignVertical="center"` and the symmetric 12pt padding keep the line
centred, and the box shrinks by the same ≈5pt as §2 below with the surface held
at 48.

**This is recorded as unmet rather than "provisionally met".**
[`smoke-verification.md` §2.1](../smoke-verification.md) condition 3 requires the
product owner to post the deferral as a comment **on this issue** before a stage
may rely on it; at the time this PR opened, issue #79 carried only the plan
comment. The build stage must not grant itself the deferral. If the owner posts
the deferral, the wording becomes exactly:

> provisionally met — the Android half of AC1 deferred to #16 by owner decision
> `<link>`

and nothing else changes.

### 2. The edge-tap consequence of decision D3 — UNMEASURED

D3: the single-line input's **own** box shrinks from 48pt (`12 + 24 + 12`) to
≈43pt (`12 + the platform's natural line at 16pt ≈ 19.1 + 12`). The field
*surface* is unaffected — the container keeps `minHeight: tokens.touch.minimum`
and `items-center` — so the visible white field is still 48pt tall and every
existing assertion stays green. What is given up is ≈2.4pt at the top and ≈2.4pt
at the bottom of the surface, where a tap lands on the container rather than on
the input.

Jest cannot compute layout in this repo, so no height-outcome assertion exists;
the 48px assertions are a **structural** pin on who owns the minimum, standing in
for the tappable-height outcome. The measurement is **UNMEASURED** — no number is
carried over from #42.

**Exact steps and threshold for the device run:**

1. Open the Stitches tab so the search field is on screen.
2. Tap ≈2pt inside the **top** edge of the field's white surface. Record whether
   the keyboard appears and the caret enters the field.
3. Dismiss the keyboard. Tap ≈2pt inside the **bottom** edge of the same
   surface. Record the same.
4. Threshold: **both taps focus the field** for the result to be "no residual".
   A miss on either is **expected-and-recorded, not a defect of #79** — it is the
   ≈5pt the input's own box gives up.

The answer decides whether the D3 follow-up is filed: raise the single-line
`paddingVertical` from `tokens.spacing[3]` (12) to `tokens.spacing[4]` (16),
giving `16 + ≈19.1 + 16 ≈ 51.1` — 48 preserved *and* an exact fit. That is
deliberately **not** in this PR: it changes the rendered height of every
single-line field in the app and contradicts this issue's own scope ("keep #42's
container minimum height, padding") and its "existing tests stay green"
criterion. It needs its own issue with its own device evidence.
